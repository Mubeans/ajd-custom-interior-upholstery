Before you call

  • The year, make and model of the vehicle — or, for a boat or a piece of furniture, what the piece is
  • Which pieces need work, and how many of them
  • Photographs of the damage or wear, taken in daylight
  • Whether you want the original material matched, or something different
  • Whether the piece can be removed and brought in, or the whole vehicle has to come
  • Ask what the shop needs before it can give you a price, and how long it would need the item
