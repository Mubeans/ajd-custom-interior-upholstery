Irving, Texas · Auto upholsterer

Cars, boats and furniture

Cars, boats and furniture are the three named in the business’s own published description of its work. This page sets out what each one covers, and the questions worth asking before you book.

— 01

Cars

Google lists the category for this business as auto upholsterer, and the business's own description names cars first.

Worth asking
  • Whether it takes on the specific piece — seat, headliner, door panel, carpet, console or trim
  • Which materials are available for your year, make and model
  • Whether the job needs the whole vehicle, or just the pieces
  • What it needs from you before it can quote, and how long it would keep the vehicle
— 02

Boats

Boats are named in the business’s own description alongside cars and furniture.

Worth asking
  • Whether it works on your type and size of boat
  • Which pieces it covers — seating, cushions, covers, panels
  • Whether marine-grade material is part of what it supplies
  • Whether the pieces come to the shop, or the shop goes to the boat
— 03

Furniture

Furniture is the third category named in the business’s own description. Its Facebook page is filed under Furniture Repair & Upholstery Service.

Worth asking
  • Whether it takes on your piece, and in what condition it needs to arrive
  • Whether you can supply your own fabric or choose from theirs
  • How the piece gets to the shop and back
  • What it needs from you before it can price the work
